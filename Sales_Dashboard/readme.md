## 📋 Summary

This Power BI dashboard delivers an executive-level view of **1K sales orders** across multiple territories and product categories from 2011 to 2014. With a **total due of $33.9M**, it tracks order status pipelines, product performance, and regional revenue distribution — all filterable by territory and year.

## 💡 Highlights

- 📦 **Bikes** are the top-selling category with 1,188 orders, followed by Components (992) and Clothing (925).
- ✅ Most orders are **Approved (396)** or **In Process (392)**, with 374 already Shipped.
- 🌍 **Canada leads revenue** at $11.94M Total Due, followed by Northwest ($6.94M) and France ($5.2M).
- 📈 Order volume shows a **strong upward trend** from 2012 to 2014 with notable spikes late in the period.
