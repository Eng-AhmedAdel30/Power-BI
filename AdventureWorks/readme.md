# 🏔️ Adventure Works Dashboard

Adventure Works Power BI Dashboard

![Dashboard](adventureworks_dashboard.png)
## 📋 Summary

This Power BI dashboard presents a comprehensive sales overview for **Adventure Works**, covering **121K orders** across multiple global territories from 2005 to 2008. With a **total due of $123.22M**, it tracks order timelines, territory performance, shipping logistics, and provides an interactive Q&A layer for ad-hoc data exploration.

Total Due$123.22M

## 💡 Highlights

- 📈 Order volume **peaked in 2007 at 51K** across all three date dimensions (Order, Due, Ship), then declined slightly in 2008.
- 🌍 **Southwest leads territorially** with 26K orders, followed by Canada (19K) and Northwest (17K).
- 🚚 Shipping is split nearly evenly between two carriers — **Cargo Transport 5 (49.79%)** and **XRQ Truck Group (50.21%)**.
- 🍁 **Canada is the top revenue territory**, with $5.44M in sales in 2006 alone — visible in the cross-tab detail table.
